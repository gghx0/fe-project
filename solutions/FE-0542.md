The zero function is not a solution, so choose $j$ with $f(j)\ne0$. From $P(j,x)$,

$$
f(f(j)^2+f(j)f(x))=j^2+xf(j).
$$

Thus $f$ is injective and surjective.

Let $h=f^{-1}$. Substituting $x=h(u)$ and $y=h(v)$ gives

$$
f(u^2+uv)=h(u)^2+uh(v).
$$

Applying $h$,

$$
h(h(u)^2+uh(v))=u^2+uv.
$$

Thus $h$ satisfies FE-0504, whose unique solution is $h(x)=x$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}.
$$
