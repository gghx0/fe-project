Setting $y=-1$ gives us $f(0)=x+f(-x)$ or $f(-x)=-x+f(0)$ or $f(x)=x+f(0)$ for all $x$. Substituting that into the original equation, we see that $x+xy+yf(0)+2f(0)=x+xy+f(0)$ for all $x,y$, meaning $f(0)=0$ or $f(x)=x$ for all $x$ (which does satisfy the original functional equation). To conclude, $f(x)=x$ for all $x$ is the only solution.
