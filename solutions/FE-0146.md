$f(f(x)+f(x)f(y))=x+yf(x)$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+f(x)f(0))$, so $f$ is surjective


Let $f(k)=-1$, then:

$P(x,k)\Rightarrow f(0)=x+kf(x)$

We can't have $k=0$ since it would imply $x=f(0)$ for all $x$, so $k\ne0$ and therefore $f$ is linear. Solutions are $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ after testing.
