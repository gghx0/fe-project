Substituting $y=1$ gives $f(2x)=f(x)f(1)+x$. Using this in original equation gives $f(xy)f(1)=f(x)f(y)$. $f(1)=0$ or $f(0)\ne 0$ then we have $f$ is constant, contradiction since $xy$ can't be constant. Therefore $f(1)\ne 0$ and $f(0)=0$. Using $f(2x)=\frac{f(x)f(2)}{f(1)}$ in first equation we get, we have $f(2)-f(1)^2\ne 0$ and $f(x)=\frac{f(1)}{f(2)-f(1)^2}x$. Direct substitution gives $f(x)=x$ for every $x$.
