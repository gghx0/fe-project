$$
f\left(x^2+f(xy)\right)=xf(x)+xy.
$$

$P(0,x)\Rightarrow f(f(0))=0$.

$P(1,f(0))\Rightarrow f(0)=0$.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2\right)=xf(x)$.

Equation now reduces to #331, $f\left(x^2+f(xy)\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+xy$, and using the result there, $f$ is an additive involution. Applying $f$ to both sides of $P(x,y)$, we have $f(xf(x)+xy)=x^2+f(xy)$ which is #439 with solutions $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$, both fit.
