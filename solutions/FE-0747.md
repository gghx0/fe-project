$f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=f(x)f(y)+xy$

If $f(a)=f(b)$:

$x=1,y=a\Rightarrow f(f(a)+f(1)f(a))=f(1)f(a)+a$

$x=1,y=b\Rightarrow f(f(a)+f(1)f(a))=f(1)f(a)+b$

So $a=b$.

We have:

$$
f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=f(x)f(y)+xy=f(yf(x)+f(x)f(y))
$$

so $xf(y)+f(x)f(y)=yf(x)+f(x)f(y)$. Setting $y=1$, this becomes $f(x)=xf(1)$, and testing, we have the only solution $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
