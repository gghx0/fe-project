This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solutions are $f(x)\equiv x$ and $f(x)\equiv -\frac{x}{2}$, which obviously work. We now show they are the only solutions. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(f(xy)+f(xy))=xf(y)+xy$. 
***
Comparing $P(x,y)$ and $P(y,x)$, $\frac{f(x)}{x}$ is constant. Checking, only $f(x)=x$ and $f(x)=-\frac{x}{2}$ work.
