From $P(0,0)$,

$$
f(0)=f(0)^2,
$$

so

$$
f(0)\in\{0,1\}.
$$

From $P(x,0)$,

$$
f(x^2)=x^2+f(0)^2.
$$

If $f(0)=1$, then

$$
f(x)=x+1
$$

for $x\ge0$.

However, $P(1,1)$ gives

$$
f(2)=1+f(1)^2,
$$

so

$$
3=5,
$$

a contradiction.

Thus

$$
f(0)=0,
$$

and

$$
f(x)=x
$$

for $x\ge0$.

From $P(0,x)$ and $P(0,-x)$,

$$
f(x)^2=f(-x)^2.
$$

Thus

$$
|f(x)|=-x
$$

for $x\le0$.

The answer set is

$$
f(x)=x\quad\text{for }x\ge0
$$

and

$$
|f(x)|=-x\quad\text{for }x\le0.
$$

To check this, notice that

$$
x^2+y^2\ge0,
$$

so

$$
f(x^2+y^2)=x^2+y^2.
$$

Also,

$$
f(y)^2=y^2,
$$

regardless of whether $f(y)=y$ or $f(y)=-y$. Hence

$$
x^2+y^2=x^2+y^2,
$$

as required.
