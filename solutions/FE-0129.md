$f(f(x)+xf(y))=x+f(xy)$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+xf(0))=x+f(0)$ so $f$ is bijective

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(f(0))=f(0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$ so the above equation becomes $f(f(x))=x$

$P(1,f(x-f(1)))\Rightarrow f(x)=x+1-f(1)$, testing we have the only solution $\boxed{f(x)=x}$
