$f\left(f(x)^2+f(x)f(y)\right)=xf(x)+yf(x)$

$\boxed{f(x)=0}$ is a solution else $f$ is surjective.

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f\left(2f(0)^2\right)=0$

$P\left(2f(0)^2,0\right)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f\left(f(x)^2\right)=xf(x)$

Equation becomes

$$
f\left(f(x)^2+f(x)f(y)\right)=f\left(f(x)^2\right)+yf(x)
$$

and since $f$ is surjective, equation reduces to:

$$
f\left(x^2+xf(y)\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+xy
$$

which is #251. Solutions $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$, and $f(x)=-x$ fits so these work.
