$f\left(x^2+xy\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+f(xy)$

$x=y=0\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

Equation is $f\left(x^2+y\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+f(y)$ whether $x=0$ or $x\ne0$ from $y\mapsto\frac yx$, hence reduced to #15, and since any additive function satisfies the equation these are the only solutions.
