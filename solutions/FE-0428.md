We claim that the unique solution is

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}.
$$

First, $f$ is injective. If $f(a)=f(b)$, then for every $x$,

$$
f(x)^2+ax=f(xf(x)+f(x)f(a))=f(xf(x)+f(x)f(b))=f(x)^2+bx.
$$

Taking $x=1$ gives $a=b$. Also $f$ is surjective, since for $x=1$ the right-hand side $f(1)^2+y$ takes every real value.

Let $h=f^{-1}$. Substituting $x=h(u)$ and $y=h(v)$ gives

$$
f(uh(u)+uv)=u^2+h(u)h(v).
$$

Applying $h$,

$$
h(u^2+h(u)h(v))=uh(u)+uv.
$$

Thus $h$ satisfies FE-0281, whose unique solution is $h(x)=x$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}.
$$
