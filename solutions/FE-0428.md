$$
f(xf(x)+f(x)f(y))=f(x)^2+xy.
$$

From this alone, $f$ is bijective: if $f(a)=f(b)$ then:

$$
f(x)^2+ax=f(xf(x)+f(x)f(a))=f(xf(x)+f(x)f(b))=f(x)^2+bx
$$

for all $x$ so $a=b$, and $f(x)^2+xy$ can take any real value for $y\in\mathbb R$.

$P\left(f^{-1}(x),f^{-1}(y)\right)\Rightarrow f\left(xf^{-1}(x)+xy\right)=x^2+f^{-1}(x)f^{-1}(y)$, so $f^{-1}$ satisfies:

$$
f^{-1}\left(x^2+f^{-1}(x)f^{-1}(y)\right)=xf^{-1}(x)+xy
$$

which is equation #281 with unique solution $f^{-1}(x)=x$, and therefore $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
