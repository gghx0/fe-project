This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x+c$ for some real constant $c$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(x+y^2)=f(x)+y^2$. 
***
$P(0,\sqrt{y}): f(y)=y+f(0)$ for all positive $y$.

For any real $x$, let $y$ be a sufficiently large positive real such that $x+y^2>0$. Then, $P(x,y)$ gives $x+y^2+f(0)=f(x)+y^2\implies f(x)+x+f(0)$.
