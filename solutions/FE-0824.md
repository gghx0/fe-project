The equation is

$$
f(2xy)=xy+f(xy).
$$

Since $xy$ ranges over all real numbers, this is equivalent to

$$
f(2t)=f(t)+t\qquad(t\in\mathbb R).
$$

For $t\ne0$, this recurrence gives

$$
f(2t)-2t=f(t)-t.
$$

Thus $f(t)-t$ is constant on each orbit of multiplication by $2$ in $\mathbb R^\ast$.

Choose arbitrary functions

$$
g:[1,2)\to\mathbb R,\qquad h:[-2,-1)\to\mathbb R,
$$

and choose an arbitrary real number $c=f(0)$. Then define

$$
f(0)=c,
$$

and for $t\ne0$, let $r(t)$ be the unique representative of the set $\{2^n t:n\in\mathbb Z\}$ lying in $[1,2)$ if $t>0$, or in $[-2,-1)$ if $t<0$. Set

$$
f(t)=
\begin{cases}
t+g(r(t)), & t>0,\\
t+h(r(t)), & t<0.
\end{cases}
$$

Every function of this form satisfies $f(2t)=f(t)+t$, and the recurrence also forces every solution to be of this form. This is the complete solution set.
