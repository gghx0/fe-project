This is a trivial problem. 
***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x+c$ for some real constant $c$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution.
***

It is easy to check that the only linear function is $f(x)=x+c$.

We now prove a more general statement.
***
**Statement: Suppose $f,g,h:\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ are functions such that for any reals $x,y$, $f(xy+g(x))=xy+h(x)$. Show that $f$ is linear.**
***
_Proof._ Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(xy+g(x))=xy+h(x)$.

$P(1,y-g(1)): f(y)=y-g(1)+h(1)$, hence $f$ is linear as desired.
