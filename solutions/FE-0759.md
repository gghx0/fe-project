Solution by **jasperE3**.
***

We claim that the only solutions are

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x\quad\text{or}\quad f(x)=-x}.
$$

Both are immediate to check. Define

$$
g(x)=\frac{f(x)+x}{2}.
$$

Then $f(x)=2g(x)-x$. Substituting this into

$$
f(xf(y)+xy)=yf(x)+xy
$$

gives

$$
2g(2xg(y))-2xg(y)=2yg(x),
$$

or equivalently

$$
g(2xg(y))=xg(y)+yg(x).
$$

This is exactly FE-0712, whose solutions are $g(x)\equiv0$ and $g(x)=x$. Translating back through $f(x)=2g(x)-x$, we get respectively

$$
\boxed{f(x)=-x}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}.
$$
