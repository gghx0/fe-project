The answer is $x\mapsto x$.

$P(-f(0),0)$ gives $f(0)^2+f(0)=f(0)$, so $f(0)=0$.
$P(x,0)$ gives $f(x^2)=x^2$, so $f(t)=t$ for $t\geqslant0$.
Now let $s<0$. Choose a negative number $x$ such that $x^2>-s$ and let $y=\frac{s}{x}\geqslant0$. We have $f(y)=y$ and $f(x^2+s)=x^2+s$, so $x^2+s=f(x^2+s)=f(x^2+xf(y))=x^2+f(xy)=x^2+f(s)$. Therefore, $f(s)=s$ and $f(t)\equiv t$.
