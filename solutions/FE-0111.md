Easy to find that $f$ is surjective.

Let

$$
f(a)=0.
$$

Then $P(x,a)$ gives

$$
f(f(x))=x.
$$

From $P(f(x),f(y))$,

$$
f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)
$$

for $y\ge0$.

Set $M\gg x,y$. Then

$$
f(M+x+y)=f(M+x)+f(y)=f(M)+f(x)+f(y).
$$

Also,

$$
f(M+x+y)=f(M)+f(x+y).
$$

Hence

$$
f(x)+f(y)=f(x+y)
$$

for all $x,y$.

Since

$$
f(f(x))=x,
$$

we get

$$
f(x)=cx.
$$

Thus $c=1$, so

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}
$$

for all $x$.
