We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(x+xf(y))=x+f(xy)$. 
***
It is easy to check that the only linear solution is $f(x)=x$.
