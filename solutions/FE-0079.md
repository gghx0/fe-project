This is a classic problem. 
***
We claim tha the only solutions are additive functions, which obviously work. A classification of additive functions can be found at [#3]({`${base}/fe/0003`}). We now show they are the only solutions. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(x+xy)=f(x)+f(xy)$. 

***
Note that $P(x,y)$ implies $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)$ for non-zero $y$.

$P(0,0): f(0)=0$. This means that $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)$ also when $y=0$.

Hence, $f$ is additive. All additive functions work.
