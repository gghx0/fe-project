$f(x)=-1$ is not a solution. Hence, there must be some $a$ such that

$$
f(a)\ne -1.
$$

Then $P(x,a)$ gives that $f$ is surjective.

Suppose

$$
f(b)\ne0.
$$

Choose $y$ such that

$$
f(y)=\frac{c}{f(b)}-1
$$

and set $x=b$. We get

$$
f(c)=\frac{bc}{f(b)}.
$$

Therefore, $f$ is linear.

Substituting

$$
f(x)=mx
$$

yields

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{f(x)=-x}
$$

as the only solutions.
