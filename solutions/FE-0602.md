Solution by **EmptySet**.
***

The only solution is the identity.

Combining $P(x, 0)$ and $P(0, x)$ (the left hand sides are equal), we get $x^2 = x\cdot f(x)$ for all $x$. This implies $f(x) = x$ for every $x \ne 0$.

If $f(0) \ne 0$, then $P(0, 0) \implies f(2\cdot f(0)) = 0$, contradicting the fact that $f$ is the identity away from zero. Thus, $f(0) = 0$, and $f$ is the identity everywhere.
