Solution by **jasperE3**.
***

Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion

$$
f(2xf(y))=xf(y)+f(xy).
$$

First,

$$
P(1,x):\quad f(2f(x))=2f(x).
$$

Then

$$
P(x,2f(y)):\quad f(4xf(y))=3xf(y)+f(xy),
$$

while

$$
P(2x,y):\quad f(4xf(y))=2xf(y)+f(2xy).
$$

Comparing these gives

$$
f(2xy)=xf(y)+f(xy).
$$

This is FE-0819, whose solutions are $f(x)=cx$. Substituting $f(x)=cx$ into the original equation gives

$$
2c^2xy=2cxy,
$$

so $c=0$ or $c=1$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{f(x)\equiv0\quad\text{or}\quad f(x)=x}.
$$
