$f(2xf(y))=xf(y)+f(xy)$.

$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(2f(x))=2f(x)$.

$P(x,2f(y))\Rightarrow f(4xf(y))=3xf(y)+f(xy)$.

$P(2x,y)\Rightarrow f(4xf(y))=2xf(y)+f(2xy)$.

Comparing, we obtain $f(2xy)=xf(y)+f(xy)$ which is #819 with solutions $f(x)=cx$. Testing, we have $\boxed{f(x)=0}$ or $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
