Answer: $f(x)=x$.

$P(0,y)$ gives us the existence of $x_0$ such that $f(x_0)=0$. $P(x,x_0)$ gives us

$$
f(x^2)=x^2,
$$

so $f(x)=x$ for all $x\ge 0$.

For some $a<0$, we pick $b>0$ such that

$$
a^2+f(a)b>0.
$$

$P(a,b)$ gives us

$$
a^2+f(a)b=a^2+ab,
$$

so $f(a)=a$ for all $a<0$.

Hence,

$$
f(x)=x
$$

for all $x\in\mathbb R$.
