This is a classic problem. 
***
The characterisation of all solutions is as follows:

Let $V=Im (f)$ be a subgroup of $(\mathbb{R},+)$. Let $S$ be a set of coset representatives for $\mathbb{R}/V$. Hence, every real number $x$ can be written uniquely as $x=s+v$, where $s\in S$ and $v\in V$. Now choose an arbitrary function $t:S\rightarrow V$ and define $f(x)=t(s)+v$.
