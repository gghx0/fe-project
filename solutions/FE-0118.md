$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+f(0))=x$ so $f$ is injective

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(2f(0))=0$

$P(2f(0),0)\Rightarrow f(f(0))=2f(0)$

$P(f(0),0)\Rightarrow f(3f(0))=f(0)\Rightarrow3f(0)=0$, so $f(0)=0$ which implies $f(f(x))=x$
Equation rewrites as:

$$
f\left(f(x)+f\left(y^2\right)\right)=x+yf(y)\Rightarrow f\left(x+f\left(y^2\right)\right)=f(x)+yf(y),
$$

which is #37 with solution $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ (since we want only the injective solutions).
