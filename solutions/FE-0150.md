$f(f(x)+f(x)f(y))=f(x)+xf(y)$

$P(-1,-1)\Rightarrow f(k)=0$ for $k=f(-1)+f(-1)^2$

$P(k,k)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x))=f(x)$

$P(f(x),y)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+f(x)f(y))=f(x)+f(x)f(y)$

Comparing with $P(x,y)$, we have $(f(x)-x)f(y)=0$, hence the two solutions $\boxed{f(x)=0}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$, easy to verify that $f(x)=0$ works and we're done
