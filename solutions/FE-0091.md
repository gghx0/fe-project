***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(f(x)+f(y))=x+f(y)$. 
***
Comparing $P(x,y)$ and $P(y,x)$, $f(x)-x$ is constant. Checking, only $f(x)=x$ works.
