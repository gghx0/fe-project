We claim the only solution is

$$
f(x)=x,
$$

which works.

From substituting $x=0$, $y=0$, we have

$$
f(0)=0.
$$

Substituting $x=-y$, we have

$$
f(-yf(-y)+yf(-y))=y^2-yf(y),
$$

or

$$
f(0)=y^2-yf(y).
$$

Thus

$$
yf(y)=y^2
$$

for all real $y$. For nonzero $y$,

$$
f(y)=y.
$$

Together with $f(0)=0$, we conclude

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}
$$

for all real $x$.
