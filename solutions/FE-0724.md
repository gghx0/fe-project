Solution by **EmptySet**.
***

Either $f(x) = x$ for all $x$ or $f(x) = -x/2$ for all $x$. Both are easily checked.

Suppose $f(u) = f(v)$. Then combining $P(1, u)$ and $P(1, v)$ (the left hand sides are equal) shows that $u = v$. So $f$ is injective.

Combining $P(x, 1)$ with $P(1, x)$ (the right hand sides are equal) shows that $f(2x\cdot f(1)) = f(2\cdot f(x))$. By injectivity, it follows that $f(x) = x\cdot f(1)$ for all $x$.

Setting $f(1)=c$, the functional equation expands to $2c^2 = 1 + c$. This is satisfied for $c=1$ and $c=-1/2$, giving us our two solutions.
