Answers: $f(x)=0$ and $f(x)=x$.

Suppose there exists a real number $a$ such that $f(a)\ne 0$ (otherwise $f(x)=0$). $P(a,y)$ implies that $f$ is bijective. $P(0,y)$ gives us $f(0)=0$. $P(x,-f(x))$ yields:

$$
xf(x)+xf(-f(x))=0.
$$

Assuming $x\ne 0$, we get

$$
f(-f(x))=-f(x).
$$

Since $-f(x)$ ranges over all real numbers by surjectivity, we conclude that

$$
f(x)=x.
$$
