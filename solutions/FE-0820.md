Substituting $y=1$ gives $f(2x)=2f(x)f(1)$ and using this in original equation gives $f(xy)f(1)f(x)f(y)$. From $f(2x)=2f(x)f(1)\Rightarrow f(2)f(x)=f(1)f(2x)=2f(x)f(1)^2$. Since $f\equiv 0$ is a solution, suppose there exsists $a$ such that $f(a)\ne 0$. Then $f(1)f(a^2)=f(a)^2\ne 0\Rightarrow f(1)\ne 0$. Hence $f(2)=2f(1)^2$. Conversely, every function with $f(xy)f(1)=f(x)f(y)$, $f(1)\ne 0$ and $f(2)=2f(1)^2$ satisfies the condition.
