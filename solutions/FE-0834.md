The equation is

$$
f(x+f(x))=2x.
$$

Define

$$
g(x)=\frac{f(x)+x}{2}.
$$

Then $f(x)=2g(x)-x$, and the equation becomes

$$
2g(2g(x))-2g(x)=2x,
$$

or

$$
g(2g(x))=x+g(x).
$$

This is exactly FE-0846. Hence the complete set of solutions is

$$
\boxed{f(x)=2g(x)-x,}
$$

where $g$ is any solution of FE-0846.
