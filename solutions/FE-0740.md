$f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=xf(y)+xy$

Since $f(x)=-2x$ isn't a solution there is some $j$ with $f(j)\ne-2j$

$P(x,zf(y)+f(y)f(z))\Rightarrow f((x+f(x))(y+f(y))z)=xyz+2xzf(y)+xf(y)f(z)$

Swapping $x,y$ and comparing, we obtain $2xzf(y)+xf(y)f(z)=2yzf(x)+yf(x)f(z)$. We can set $y=1$ and $z=j$ to reduce this to $f(x)=xf(1)$, testing, our solutions are $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
