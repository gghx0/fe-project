Solution by **SMOJ**.
***

We claim the only solutions are $f(x)\equiv x$ and $f(x)\equiv -x$, which obviously work. We now show they are the only solutions. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(xf(y)+x)=xy+f(x)$. 
***
$P(1,y)$ gives $f(f(y)+1)=y+f(1)$.

Now $P(f(x)+1,y)$ gives $f((f(y)+1)(f(x)+1))=yf(x)+y+x+f(1)$.

Swapping $x$ and $y$, $yf(x)=xf(y)$ for all $x,y$, hence $f(x)=cx$ for some real constant $c$. Checking, only $c=1$ and $c=-1$ works.
