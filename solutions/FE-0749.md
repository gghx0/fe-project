$f((x+f(x))f(y))=2xy$

$f$ is injective, since if $f(a)=f(b)$ then $P\left(\frac12,a\right)$ and $P\left(\frac12,b\right)$ give $a=b$.
Swap $x,y$, we obtain:

$$
f((x+f(x))f(y))=2xy=f((y+f(y))f(x)),
$$

so $(x+f(x))f(y)=(y+f(y))f(x)$. Setting $y=1$, this simplifies as $f(x)=xf(1)$ and we can test to get the only solution $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ (since $1$ is the unique root or $r^3+r^2-2=0$).
