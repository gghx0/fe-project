$f\left(x^2+f\left(y^2\right)\right)=x^2+f\left(y^2\right)$.

Set $c=\inf_{x\ge0}f(x)$. If $c<0$ then there is some $k\ge0$ with $f(k)<0$, then:

$P\left(\sqrt{k-f(k)},\sqrt k\right)\Rightarrow f(k)=k$, contradiction.

So $c\ge0$, equation is $f(x)=x$ for all $x\ge f\left(y^2\right)$ and it's clear now that the solutions are:

$\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\ge c\\g(x)&\text{if }0\le x<c\\h(x)&\text{if }x<0\end{cases}}$

where $c\ge0$ is any constant and $g:[0,c)\to[c,\infty)$ and $h:\mathbb R^-\to\mathbb R$ are any functions.

$\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x>c\\g(x)&\text{if }0\le x\le c\\h(x)&\text{if }x<0\end{cases}}$

where $c\ge0$ is any constant and $g:[0,c]\to(c,\infty)$ and $h:\mathbb R^-\to\mathbb R$ are any functions.
