$$
f\left(x^2+f(y)^2\right)=f(x)^2+y^2.
$$

Note that:

$$
f\left(f\left(x^2+f(y)^2\right)\right)=f\left(y^2+f(x)^2\right)=x^2+f(y)^2,
$$

so $f(f(x))=x$ for all $x\ge f(0)^2$. For $y\ge f(0)$:

$P(f(y),x)\Rightarrow f\left(f(x)^2+f(y)^2\right)=x^2+y^2$.

$P(f(x),y)\Rightarrow f\left(f(x)^2+f(y)^2\right)=f(f(x))^2+y^2$ so we obtain $f(f(x))^2=x^2$ for all $x$.

In particular, $f(f(0))=0$.

$P(0,f(0))\Rightarrow f(0)=2f(0)^2\Rightarrow f(0)\in\left\{\frac12,0\right\}$.

Suppose $f(0)=\frac12$, then $f\left(\frac12\right)=0$ and so:

$P\left(1,\frac12\right)\Rightarrow f(1)=f(1)^2+\frac14\Rightarrow f(1)=\frac12$.

$P(0,1)\Rightarrow f\left(\frac14\right)=\frac54$.

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f\left(\frac14\right)=\frac14$, contradiction.

So $f(0)=0$.

$P(x,f(y))\Rightarrow f\left(x^2+y^2\right)=f(x)^2+f(y)^2$, the equation reduces to the $f(0)=0$ case of #182 which has the solution:

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\ge0\\xe(x)&\text{if }x<0\end{cases}}
$$

where $e:\mathbb R^-\to\{-1,1\}$ is any function. To see that these satisfy the equation, note that $f(x)^2=x^2$ and so:

$$
f\left(x^2+f(y)^2\right)=f\left(x^2+y^2\right)=x^2+y^2=f(x)^2+y^2.
$$
