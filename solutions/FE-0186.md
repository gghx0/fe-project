$f\left(x^2+yf(y)\right)=x^2+yf(y)$.

If $xf(x)$ is unbounded below, then $f(x)=x$ for all $x$, contradiction. So let $c=\inf_{x\in\mathbb R}xf(x)$, it's clear that $f(x)=x$ for all $x>c$ and that $c\le0$. So our solutions are as follows:

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\ge c\\\frac cx+g(x)&\text{if }x<c\end{cases}}
$$

where $g:(-\infty,c)\to(-\infty,0]$ is any function and $c\le0$ any constant, which fits.
