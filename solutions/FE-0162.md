Answers: $f(x)=0$ and $f(x)=x$.

Let $a\ne 0$. $P\left(a,\frac{a-f(a)}{a}\right)$ gives us the existence of $x_0$ such that $f(x_0)=0$. $P(x_0,y)$ gives us

$$
f(x_0y)=0,
$$

so either $f(x)=0$ for all $x\in\mathbb R$, or $f(t)=0$ implies $t=0$.

From $P\left(a,\frac{a-f(a)}{a}\right)$, we get

$$
f(a)f\left(\frac{a-f(a)}{a}\right)=0,
$$

which means $f(a)=a$ for all real numbers.
