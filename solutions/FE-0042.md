This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(x+xf(y))=x+f(x)f(y)$. 
***
$P(0,y): f(0)=f(y)f(0)$. Obviously $f$ cannot be identically $1$, hence $f(0)=0$.

$P(x,0): f(x)=x$.
