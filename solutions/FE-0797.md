This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solutions are $f(x)\equiv x$, $f(x)\equiv -x$, and $f(x)\equiv 0$, which obviously work. We now show they are the only solutions. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(f(x)f(y)+xy)=xf(y)+f(xy)$. 
***
Comparing $P(x,y)$ and $P(y,x)$, $\frac{f(x)}{x}$ is constant. Checking, only $f(x)=x$, $f(x)=-x$, and $f(x)=0$ work.
