Equation is $f(f(x)+f(y))=f(x)+y$ for all $y\ge0$.

Comparing $P(x,0)$ and $P(0,x)$, we get $f(x)=x+f(0)$ for all $x\ge0$, so equation becomes $f(f(x)+y+f(0))=f(x)+y$. Take $y$ large enough so that $y,f(x)+y+f(0)\ge0$, then $f(0)=0$. We can prove that the remaining system $f(x+f(y))=x+f(y)$ for all $x\ge0$ and $f(x)=x$ for all $x\ge0$ is equivalent to the original equation, so our solutions are:
$f(x)=x$

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\ge c\\g(x)&\text{if }x<c\end{cases}
$$

where $c\le0$ and $g:(-\infty,c)\to[c,\infty)$
