$f\left(x^2+yf(x)\right)=x^2+xf(y)$

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(x)=x$ for all $x\ge0$

$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(x+1)=f(x)+1$ for all $x$, which implies $f(x+n)=f(x)+n$ for all $n\in\mathbb{N}$ by induction. For any $x$, take $n$ large 
enough such that $x+n\ge0$, we obtain $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
