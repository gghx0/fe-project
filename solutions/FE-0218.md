$f\left(x^2+f\left(y^2\right)\right)=x^2+yf(y)$.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2+f(0)\right)=x^2$, so $f(x)=x-f(0)$ for all $x\ge0$.

Fix $y$ and let $x>\sqrt{\left\lvert f\left(y^2\right)\right\rvert}$:

$P(x,y)\Rightarrow x^2+f\left(y^2\right)-f(0)=x^2+yf(y)$.

So we obtain $f\left(y^2\right)=yf(y)+f(0)$ for all $y$.

With $y=1$, this becomes $f(0)=0$, and with $y\mapsto-y$ we obtain that $f$ is odd. From before $f(x)=x-f(0)=x$ for all $x\ge0$, and so our solution is $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
