The only solutions are $f(x)\equiv 0$ and $f(x)\equiv x$, which obviously work.

**Claim.** $f(x) = 0$ or $x$ pointwise.

**Proof.** Fix any $x\in\mathbb{R}$. If $f(x)\neq x$, then $P\left(x, \frac{x^2}{x-f(x)}\right)$ gives $f(x)^2 = 0$, so $f(x) = 0$.

Suppose $f(x) = 0$ for some $x\neq 0$. Then $P(x, y/x)$ gives $f(x^2) = f(y)$, so $f$ is constant and therefore 0.
