$f(xy+xf(y))=2xy$

Note that $f(x)=-x$ isn't a solution, so there is some $j$ with $f(j)\ne-j$

Set $x\mapsto\frac x{j+f(j)}$ and $y=j$, then:

$$
f(x)=\frac{2xj}{j+f(j)},
$$

and testing, we have the solutions $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=-2x}$.
