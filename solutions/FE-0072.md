This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(x+xy)=x+f(x)f(y)$. 
***
$P(0,y): f(0)=f(0)f(y)$. If $f(0)\ne 0$, then $f(y)=1$, which is not a solution. Hence, $f(0)=0$.

$P(x,0): f(x)=x$.
