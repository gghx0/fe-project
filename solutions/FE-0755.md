***

We claim the only solutions are $f(x)\equiv x$ and $f(x)\equiv -x$, which obviously work. We now show they are the only solutions. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(xf(y)+xy)=xf(y)+xy$. 
***
Case 1: $f(y)+y=0$ for all $y$, which implies $f(x)=-x$.

Case 2: $f(u)+u\ne 0$ for some real $u$.

Then, $P(\frac{x}{f(u)+u},u): f(x)=x$.
