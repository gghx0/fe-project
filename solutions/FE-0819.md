This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv cx$ for some real constant $c$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(xy+xy)=xf(y)+f(xy)$. 
***
Comparing $P(x,y)$ and $P(y,x)$, $\frac{f(x)}{x}$ is constant.
