Equation is $f(f(x)+f(y))=f(x)+y$ for all $x>0$.

If $f(a)=f(b)$, then $P(1,a)$ and $P(1,b)$ give $a=b$, so $f$ is injective.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+f(0))=f(x)\Rightarrow f(x)=x-f(0)$ for all $x>0$

Equation becomes $f(x+f(y)-f(0))=x+y-f(0)$ for all $x>0$.

Take $y>\max\{0,2f(0)-x\}$, this becomes:

$$
x+y-3f(0)=f(x+y-2f(0))=f(x+f(y)-f(0))=x+y-f(0),
$$

so $f(0)=0$

Equation becomes $f(x+f(y))=x+y$ for all $x>0$, which is #316 whose only solution is $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
