$f\left(f(x)^2+yf(x)\right)=x^2+xf(y)$

$P(0,-f(0))\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

$P(x,-f(x))\Rightarrow x^2+xf(-f(x))=0\Rightarrow f(-f(x))=-x$ for all $x\ne0$, but also true for $x=0$.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f\left(f(x)^2\right)=x^2$

Sub $x\mapsto f(x)^2$ into $f(-f(x))=-x$, we get $f\left(-x^2\right)=-f(x)^2$, and sub $x\mapsto-f(x)$ into $f\left(f(x)^2\right)=x^2$, we get $f\left(x^2\right)=f(x)^2$, so $f$ is odd. Thus $f(f(x))=x$, equation reduces to #305. Checking, only $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ works.
