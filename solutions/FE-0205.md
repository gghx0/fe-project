$P(x,y):f\left(x^2+f(y)^2\right)=xf(x)+y^2$
$P(0,x)\Rightarrow f\left(f(x)^2\right)=x^2\Rightarrow f\left(x^4\right)=f(x)^4$, we also have $f\left(f(0)^2\right)=0$
$P\left(1,f(0)^2\right)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$
$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2\right)=xf(x)$
So:

$$
f(x)^4=f\left(x^4\right)=x^2f\left(x^2\right)=x^3f(x)
$$

which implies $f(x)\in\{0,x\}$ for each $x$.
The equation $f\left(f(x)^2\right)=x^2$ eliminates $f(x)=0$ for $x\ne0$, so $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ for all $x$
