$f(f(x)+f(x)f(y))=x+f(x)f(y)$

$P(x,f(z)+f(z)f(y))\Rightarrow f(f(x)+zf(x)+f(x)f(y)f(z))=x+zf(x)+f(x)f(y)f(z)$
In particular, setting $z=-1$ forces

$$
f(f(x)f(y)f(-1))=x-f(x)+f(x)f(y)f(-1)
$$

and swapping $x,y$:

$$
x-f(x)+f(x)f(y)f(-1)=f(f(x)f(y)f(-1))=y-f(y)+f(x)f(y)f(-1),
$$

so $x-f(x)$ is constant, i.e. $f$ is linear. Testing, we have the only solution $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
