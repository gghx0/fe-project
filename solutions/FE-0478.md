***

Switching $x$ and $y$, we get that

$$
f(x^2)-x^2
$$

is a constant $c$. This means that

$$
f(x)=x+c
$$

for all $x\ge0$.

Therefore,

$$
f(x)^2+f(y)^2+c=x^2+y^2+c
$$

for all $x$ and $y$. Therefore,

$$
f(x)^2-x^2=-f(0).
$$

This means that

$$
f(x)=\pm\sqrt{x^2-f(0)}
$$

for all $x$.

Considering $x\ge0$, we get

$$
f(0)=0
$$

and

$$
f(x)=x.
$$

However, for negative values, we see that $f(x)$ can be chosen to be either

$$
+x
$$

or

$$
-x.
$$
