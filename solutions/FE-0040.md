This is a trivial problem.
***

We claim the only solution is $f(x)\equiv x$, which obviously works. We now show it is the only solution. Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion that $f(x+xf(y))=x+xf(y)$.
***
Obviously $f\equiv -1$ does not work. Let $u$ be a real constant such that $f(u)\ne -1$. Then, $P(\frac{x}{f(u)+1},u): f(x)=x$.
