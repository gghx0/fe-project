$P(x,-x)\Rightarrow f(-x)(f(x)-x)=0$, so using the above fact, for all $x$ either $f(x)=x$ or (in the case $f(-x)=0$) $f(x)=0$.

$P(a,b)\Rightarrow f\left(a^2+ab\right)=a^2\in\left\{0,a^2+ab\right\}$
