$f\left(x^2+xy\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+f(x)f(y)$

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(x+1)=f(1)f(x)+f(1)$

$P(1,-1)\Rightarrow f(1)(1+f(-1))=0$

If $f(1)=0$, then $P(1,x-1)\Rightarrow\boxed{f(x)=0}$ which is a solution

Otherwise assume $f(-1)=-1$

$P(1,1)\Rightarrow f(2)=f(1)^2+f(1)$

$P(-1,2)\Rightarrow f(1)\in\{1,-1\}$
***
**Case 1:** $f(1)=1$


$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(x+1)=f(x)+1$

$P(x,y+1)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2+xy+x\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+f(x)f(y)+f(x)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2+xy+x\right)=f\left(x^2+xy\right)+f(x)$

For $x\ne0$, $x^2+xy$ can take any value, so $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)$ for all $x\ne0$, but it's also true for $x=0$.

Original equation becomes $f(xy)=f(x)f(y)$ and it's well-known that the only not allzero additive and multiplicative function is $\boxed{f(x)=x}$
***

**Case 2:** $f(1)=-1$

$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(x+1)=-f(x)-1\Rightarrow f(x+2)=-f(x+1)-1=f(x)$

$P(x,y+2)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2+xy+2x\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+f(x)f(y)=f\left(x^2+xy\right)$

For $x\ne0$, $x^2+xy$ can take any value, so $f(2x+y)=f(y)$ for all $x\ne0$, but it's also true for $x=0$. Therefore $f$ is constant, no further solutions appear after testing.
