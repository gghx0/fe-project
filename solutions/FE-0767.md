We claim the answers are $f(x)=0$ and $f(x)=x$, which both work.

Assume $f(y)\ne 0$ for some $y$, otherwise we get the first solution. Then vary $x$ giving $f$ surjective. Now $P(1,y)$ gives $f(2f(y))=2f(y)$. Since $2f(y)$ can take any real value by surjectivity, we get $f(x)=x$ for all $x$ as desired.
