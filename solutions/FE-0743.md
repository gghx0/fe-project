**Claim:** $f(x)+x$ is injective

If $a+f(a)=b+f(b)$:

$P(a,k)\Rightarrow f((a+f(a))f(k))=f(a)(k+f(k))$

$P(b,k)\Rightarrow f((a+f(a))f(k))=f(b)(k+f(k))$

and so $f(a)=f(b)$ and so $a=b$
***
Using this, we have:

$$
xf(y)+f(x)f(y)+f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=xf(y)+yf(x)+2f(x)f(y),
$$

and swapping $x,y$ this implies:

$$
xf(y)+f(x)f(y)+f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=yf(x)+f(x)f(y)+f(yf(x)+f(x)f(y)).
$$

Now we can use the injectivity of $f(x)+x$ to obtain:

$$
xf(y)+f(x)f(y)=yf(x)+f(x)f(y)
$$

which implies ($y=1$) that $f(x)=xf(1)$ for all $x$. Testing, our only remaining solutions are $\boxed{f(x)=0}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
