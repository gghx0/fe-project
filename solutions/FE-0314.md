Let $P(x, y)$ denote $f(x^{2} + f(xy)) = x^{2} + yf(x)$.

$P(0, 0) : f(f(0)) = 0$

$P(0, 1) : f(0) = 0$

$P(x, 0) : f(x^{2}) = x^{2}$

$P(x, x) : f(x) = x$ for all $x \neq 0$.

Since $f(0) = 0$, we have that $f(x) \equiv x$, which is a solution.
