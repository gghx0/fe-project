$f(f(x)+xf(y))=f(x)+f(xy)$.

$P(-1,-1)\Rightarrow f(0)=f(-1)+f(1)$.

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(f(0))=2f(0)$.

$P(1,-1)\Rightarrow f(f(0))=f(0)$.

So $f(0)=0$, from the above we get $f(-1)=-f(1)$.

Suppose $f(k)=0$ for some $k\ne0$:

$P(x,k),P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(xk)=0$ so $\boxed{f(x)=0}$ for all $x$ which is indeed a solution.

Otherwise, assume $f(x)\ne0$ for all $x\ne0$.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x))=f(x)$, in particular $f(-f(1))=-f(1)$.

$P(f(1),-1)\Rightarrow f(f(1)+f(1)f(-1))=0\Rightarrow f(1)+f(1)f(-1)=0$, combined with $f(-1)=-f(1)$ we have $f(1)=1$ (since $f(1)\ne0$) and $f(-1)=-1$.

$P(-1,x)\Rightarrow f(-1-f(x))=-1+f(-x)$.

$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(1+f(x))=1+f(x)$.

$P(1+f(x),-1)\Rightarrow f(-f(x))=-1-f(x)$

so $f$ is odd.

$P(x,-1)\Rightarrow f(f(x)-x)=0\Rightarrow\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
