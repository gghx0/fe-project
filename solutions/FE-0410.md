Solution by **jasperE3**.
***

Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion

$$
f(xf(x)+yf(x))=f(x^2)+xf(y).
$$

First,

$$
P(-1,1):\quad f(0)=f(1)-f(1)=0.
$$

Next, applying $P(-x,x)$ gives

$$
f(0)=f(x^2)-xf(x).
$$

Since $f(0)=0$, we have

$$
f(x^2)=xf(x).
$$

Therefore the original equation becomes

$$
f(xf(x)+yf(x))=xf(x)+xf(y),
$$

which is exactly FE-0400. Hence the solutions are

$$
\boxed{f(x)\equiv0\quad\text{or}\quad f(x)=x}.
$$
