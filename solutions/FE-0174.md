Solution by **jasperE3**.
***

Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion

$$
f(f(x)+f(xy))=f(x)+f(xy).
$$

For $x\ne0$, replacing $y$ by $y/x$ gives

$$
f(f(x)+f(y))=f(x)+f(y).
$$

For $x=0$, the same identity follows from $P(y,0)$:

$$
f(f(y)+f(0))=f(y)+f(0).
$$

Therefore this equation is equivalent to FE-0092. Hence the solutions are exactly the functions satisfying the FE-0092 condition: if

$$
A=f(\mathbb R),
$$

then

$$
A+A\subseteq A
$$

and

$$
f(t)=t
$$

for every $t\in A+A$.

Conversely, any function with this property clearly satisfies the original equation.
