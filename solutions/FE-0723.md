We claim the solutions are precisely $f(x) \equiv x$ and $f(x) \equiv -x$. It is clear these work.

Clearly, $f(x) \equiv 0$ fails by choosing $x, y \neq 0$, so assume there is some constant $k \in \mathbb{R}$ for which $f(k) = r \neq 0$. Then $f(2rx) = 2kx$ for any $x \in \mathbb{R}$, so $f(x) = cx$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$, where $c = \frac{k}{r}$ is fixed. We then have $2c^2 = f(2c) = 2$, so $c = \pm 1$, as desired.
