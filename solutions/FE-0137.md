$f(f(x)+yf(x))=x+f(x)f(y)$

This is trivial.

Plug in $y=-1$, the equation becomes $f(0)=x+f(x)f(-1)$. If $f(-1)=0$ this is $x=f(0)$ for all $x$, obviously false, so $f(-1)\ne0$ and so $f$ is linear. Our solutions after testing are $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$.
