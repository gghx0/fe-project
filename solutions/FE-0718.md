$f(2xf(y))=xy+yf(x)$

$P(x,2yf(z))\Rightarrow f(2xyz+2xzf(y))=2xyf(z)+2yf(x)f(z)$

Swap $x,z$, we get $2xyf(z)=2zyf(x)$ for all $x,y,z$, so $f(x)=xf(1)$ (setting $y=z=1$)

Testing, our solutions are $\boxed{f(x)=-\frac12x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$
