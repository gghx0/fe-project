$$
f\left(x^2+f(xy)\right)=f\left(x^2\right)+xy.
$$
Equation implies $f(x+f(y))=f(x)+y$ for all $x>0$, call this $P(x,y)$.

For all sufficiently large $x$, we have:
$$
f(x+f(y+z))\overset{P(x,y+z)}=f(x)+y+z\overset{P(x,y)}=f(x+f(y))+z\overset{P(x+f(y),z)}=f(x+f(y)+f(z)),
$$
so $f(x+f(y+z)-f(y)-f(z))=f(x)$ for all $x>\max\{-f(y)-f(z),-f(y)-2f(z)\}$. Moreover:
$$
P(1,x+f(y+z)-f(y)-f(z))\Rightarrow f(1+f(x))=f(1)+x+f(y+z)-f(y)-f(z),
$$
so (comparing with $P(1,x)$) $f(y+z)=f(y)+f(z)$ for all $y,z\in\mathbb R$, that is, $f$ is additive.

Then $P(x,y)$ becomes $f(f(y))=y$ for all $y$, so $f$ is an additive involution. Easy to check that any additive involution fits, so $\boxed{f(x)=v_1(x)-v_2(x)}$ where $V_1,V_2$ are two $\mathbb Q$-supplementary subvectorspaces of the $\mathbb Q$-vectorspace $\mathbb R$ and $v_1:\mathbb R\to V_1$ and $v_2:\mathbb R\to V_2$ the projections of a real in $V_1,V_2$.
