$$
f(xf(x)+f(xy))=xf(x)+xf(y).
$$

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(f(0))=0$.

$P(f(0),0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(xf(x))=xf(x)$.

$P(1,xf(x))\Rightarrow f(xf(x)+f(1))=xf(x)+f(1)$.

$P\left(x,\frac1x\right)\Rightarrow f(xf(x)+f(1))=xf(x)+xf\left(\frac1x\right)$.

Comparing, we obtain $xf\left(\frac1x\right)=f(1)$, so $f(x)=xf(1)$ for all $x\ne0$. Combined with $f(0)=0$, this is true for all $x$, and testing, we have solutions $\boxed{f(x)=0}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$.
