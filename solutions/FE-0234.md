The answer is $f(x)\equiv x$ only. 

$P(0,0)$ gives $f(0)=0$.

$P(x, 0)$ gives $f(x^2)=x^2$, i.e. $f(t)=t$ for all $t\geqslant0$.

Now let $y$ be an arbitrary number. For some sufficiently large positive number $x_0$ we have $x_0^2+x_0f(y)>0$. Then $P(x_0, y)$ gives $x_0^2+yx_0=x_0^2+yf(x_0)=f(x_0^2+x_0f(y))=x_0^2+x_0f(y)$, so $y=f(y)$ for all $y$.
