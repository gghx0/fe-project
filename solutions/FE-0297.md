This is trivial. Let

$$
g(x)=f(x)-x.
$$

Then the equation becomes

$$
g(x^2+xy)=g(xy).
$$

For any real numbers $a>b$, plug

$$
x=\sqrt{a-b}
$$

and

$$
y=\frac{b}{x}.
$$

Then

$$
g(a)=g(b),
$$

hence $g$ is constant.

Therefore

$$
f(x)=x+c,
$$

which works.
