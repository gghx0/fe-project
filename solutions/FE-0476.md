The answer is $f(x)\equiv x$.
$P(y,x)$ gives $x^2+yf(y)=y^2+xf(x)$. Taking $x=0$, we obtain $yf(y)=y^2$, so $f(y)=y$ for all $y\neq0$.

Suppose that $f(0)\neq0$, so $2f(0)^2\neq0$. Then $P(0,0)$ gives $0=f(2f(0)^2)=2f(0)^2\neq0$. A contradiction. Therefore, $f(x)=x$ for all $x$.
