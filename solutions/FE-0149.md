$f(f(x)+f(x)f(y))=x+f(xy)$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+f(x)f(0))=x+f(0)$, so $f$ is surjective

Let $f(k)=0$:

$P(k,0)\Rightarrow k=0$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(f(x))=x$

$P(x,f(y))\Rightarrow f(f(x)+yf(x))=x+f(xf(y))$

Set $y=-1$, we obtain $x+f(xf(-1))=0$, so since $f$ is bijective $f(-1)\ne0$ and so $f$ is linear. Testing, our solutions are $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$.
