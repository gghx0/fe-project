Equation is $f(y+xf(x))=f(y)+xf(x)$ for all $x\ne0$, but it also holds for $x=0$. By #21, solutions are $\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}0&\text{if }x\ne0\\c&\text{if }x=0\end{cases}}$ for a constant $c\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x+c}$ for a constant $c\in\mathbb{R}$
