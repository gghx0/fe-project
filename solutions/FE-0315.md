Either

$$
f(x)=x
$$

or

$$
f(x)=|x|.
$$

Both are easily verified.

From $P(0,u)$,

$$
f(f(0))=f(0)f(u).
$$

Because $f$ cannot be constant, this implies

$$
f(0)=0.
$$

From $P(u,0)$,

$$
f(u^2)=u^2,
$$

so $f$ is the identity on the nonnegative numbers.

From $P(u,u)$,

$$
u^2=f(u)^2,
$$

so for $u<0$, $f(u)$ equals either $u$ or $|u|$.

If $u,v<0$, then $P(u,v)$ gives

$$
uv=f(u)f(v),
$$

implying that the sign of $f$ is constant on the negative reals.

Therefore the solutions are

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x}
$$

and

$$
\boxed{f(x)=|x|}.
$$
