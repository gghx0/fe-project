This equation implies Cauchy's equation with the additional constraint that $f$ is an involution. See #6 for a classification.

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow$

$$
f(2\cdot f(0))=0.
$$

$P(2\cdot f(0),2\cdot f(0))\Rightarrow$

$$
f(0)=4\cdot f(0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0.
$$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow$

$$
f(f(x))=x.
$$

$P(f(a),f(b))\Rightarrow$

$$
f(a+b)=f(a)+f(b).
$$
