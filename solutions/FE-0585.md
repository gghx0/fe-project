Solution by **jasperE3**.
***

We use the observation from FE-0459: if

$$
f(g(x)+f(xy))=x^2+f(xy),
$$

then $g(x)-x^2$ is constant for $x\ne0$, and $f(t)-t$ is eventually constant.

Here $g(x)=f(x)^2$. Hence $f(x)^2-x^2$ is constant for $x\ne0$. Since $f(t)-t$ is eventually constant, say $f(t)=t+b$ for all large $t$, we get

$$
(t+b)^2-t^2
$$

constant for all large $t$, forcing $b=0$. Therefore

$$
f(x)^2=x^2\qquad(x\ne0).
$$

The equation reduces to FE-0317:

$$
f(x^2+f(xy))=x^2+f(xy).
$$

Combining the FE-0317 classification with $f(x)=\pm x$ for $x\ne0$ gives the following possibilities:

$$
\boxed{f(x)=x},
$$

$$
\boxed{f(x)=|x|},
$$

and, for any constant $c<0$,

$$
\boxed{
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
x,&x>c,\\
-x,&x\le c,
\end{cases}}
$$

or

$$
\boxed{
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
x,&x\ge c,\\
-x,&x<c.
\end{cases}}
$$

The listed functions all satisfy $f(x)^2=x^2$ for every $x$ and belong to the FE-0317 solution family, so they work.
