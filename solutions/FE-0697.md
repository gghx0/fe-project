$f\left(f\left(x^2\right)+f(xy)\right)=xf(x)+yf(x)$

$P(0,x)\Rightarrow f(2f(0))=xf(0)$, varying $x$ we see that the only possibility for $f(0)$ is $f(0)=0$

For $x,y\ne0$ we have:

$$
P\left(x,\frac{y^2}x\right)\Rightarrow f\left(f\left(x^2\right)+f\left(y^2\right)\right)=xf(x)+\frac{y^2f(x)}x,
$$

and swapping $x,y$:

$$
xf(x)+\frac{y^2f(x)}x=yf(y)+\frac{x^2f(y)}y.
$$

Setting $y=1$, this is $f(x)=xf(1)$ which is also true for $x=0$, hence $f$ is at most linear. Testing such solutions we find that $\boxed{f(x)=0}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ are the solutions.
