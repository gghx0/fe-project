$$
f(xf(x)+f(xy))=xf(x)+yf(x).
$$

$P(-1,1)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$.

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f(xf(x))=xf(x)$.

$P(1,xf(x))\Rightarrow f(xf(x)+f(1))=xf(x)f(1)+f(1)$.

$P\left(x,\frac1x\right)\Rightarrow f(xf(x)+f(1))=xf(x)+\frac{f(x)}x$.

Comparing, we obtain $f(x)\left(x^2-x^2f(1)+1\right)=xf(1)$ for all $x\ne0$, but it's clearly also true for $x=0$. Setting $x=1$, this is $f(1)\in\{0,1\}$. If $f(1)=0$ this simplifies to $f(x)\left(x^2+1\right)=0$ and so $\boxed{f(x)=0}$,while if $f(1)=1$ this simplifies to $\boxed{f(x)=x}$, easy to verify that both are solutions.
