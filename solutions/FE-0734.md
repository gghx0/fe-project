Let $P(x,y)$ be the assertion $f(xf(y)+yf(x))=2xy$.

**Claim:** $f$ is injective.

Let $f(a)=f(b)$.

$P(a,a)\Rightarrow f(2af(a))=2a^2$.

$P(b,b)\Rightarrow f(2bf(b))=2b^2$.

$P(a,b)\Rightarrow f(af(b)+bf(a))=2ab$.

$P(2af(a),2bf(b))\Rightarrow f\left(4a^2bf(b)+4ab^2f(a)\right)=8abf(a)^2$.

$P(af(b)+bf(a),af(b)+bf(a))\Rightarrow f\left(4a^2bf(b)+4ab^2f(a)\right)=2(a+b)^2f(a)^2$.

Comparing, we obtain $(a+b)^2f(a)^2=4abf(a)^2$. Now if $f(a)=f(b)=0$ then $P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$, and our expressions for $P(a,a)$ and $P(b,b)$ give $a=b=0$. On the other hand, if $f(a)\ne0$ then $(a+b)^2=4ab$ and we again have $a=b$. So in either case $f(a)=f(b)$ implies $a=b$.

The rest is easier.

$P\left(\frac12,\frac12\right)\Rightarrow f\left(f\left(\frac12\right)\right)=\frac12$.

$P\left(f\left(\frac12\right),f\left(\frac12\right)\right)\Rightarrow\frac12=2f\left(\frac12\right)^2$, so $f\left(\frac12\right)=\pm\frac12$. Likewise:

$P\left(-\frac12,-\frac12\right)\Rightarrow f\left(-f\left(-\frac12\right)\right)=\frac12$, by injectivity $f\left(-\frac12\right)=-f\left(\frac12\right)$ and so either $f\left(\frac12\right)=\frac12$, $f\left(-\frac12\right)=-\frac12$ or $f\left(\frac12\right)=-\frac12$, $f\left(-\frac12\right)=\frac12$. Notice that $f(x)$ is a solution iff $f(-x)$ is, so WLOG we can have the first option $f\left(\frac12\right)=\frac12$ and $f\left(-\frac12\right)=-\frac12$.

$P\left(x,\frac12\right)\Rightarrow f\left(\frac{x+f(x)}2\right)=x$, taking $x\mapsto f(x)$ here we have:
$$
f\left(\frac{f(x)+f(f(x))}2\right)=f(x)\Rightarrow\frac{f(x)+f(f(x))}2=x\Rightarrow f(f(x))=2x-f(x).
$$
Finally:

$$
\begin{align*}
4x^2-4xf(x)+2f(x)^2&=2xf(f(x))+2f(x)^2-2xf(x)\\&\overset{P(x,f(x))}=2xf(f(x))+2f(x)^2-f\left(xf(f(x))+f(x)^2\right)\\&=f\left(f\left(xf(f(x))+f(x)^2\right)\right)\\&\overset{P(x,f(x))}=f(2xf(x))\\&\overset{P(x,x)}=2x^2
\end{align*}
$$
which simplifies to $2(x-f(x))^2=0$, i.e. $\boxed{f(x)=x}$. The solution $f(-x)$ is $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$.
