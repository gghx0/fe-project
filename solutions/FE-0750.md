$f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=xy+f(xy)$

$P(1,x)\Rightarrow f(f(x)+f(1)f(x))=x+f(x)$, so $f$ is injective

Swapping $x,y$ we have:

$$
f(xf(y)+f(x)f(y))=xy+f(xy)=f(yf(x)+f(x)f(y))
$$

so by injectivity $xf(y)=yf(x)$ which implies that the solutions are of the form $f(x)=xf(1)$. Testing, we have solutions $\boxed{f(x)=x}$ and $\boxed{f(x)=-x}$.
