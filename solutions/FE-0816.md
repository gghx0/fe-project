***

Let $P(x,y)$ denote the assertion $f(2xy)=xf(y)+yf(x)$.

$P\left(x,\frac12\right)$ gives

$$
f(x)=xf\left(\frac12\right)+\frac12 f(x),
$$

so $f(x)=2f\left(\frac12\right)x$ for all $x$.

Conversely, every function of the form $f(x)=cx$ works by direct substitution.
