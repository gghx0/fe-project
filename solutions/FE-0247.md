$f\left(x^2+xf(y)\right)=f(x)^2+f(xy)$

$P(0,0)\Rightarrow f(0)=0$

$P(x,0)\Rightarrow f\left(x^2\right)=f(x)^2$

If $f(k)=0$ for some $k\ne0$:

$P(x,k)\Rightarrow f(xk)=0\Rightarrow\boxed{f(x)=0}$ which is a solution

Else, $f(x)\ne0$ for all $x\ne0$

$P(f(x)-x,x)\Rightarrow f(x-f(x))=0\Rightarrow\boxed{f(x)=x}$
