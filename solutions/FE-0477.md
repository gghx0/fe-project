$f\left(f(x)^2+f(y)^2\right)=x^2+f(y)^2$

From $P(x,0)$ and $P(0,x)$ we have $f(x)^2=x^2+f(0)^2$.

Applying this to $P(0,0)$ we have:

$$
f\left(2f(0)^2\right)^2=f(0)^2\Rightarrow 4f(0)^4+f(0)^2=f(0)^4\Rightarrow f(0)=0.
$$

So $f(x)\in\{-x,x\}$. Original equation now implies $f\left(x^2+y^2\right)=x^2+y^2$, i.e. $f(x)=x$ for all $x\ge0$ so our solutions are

$$
\boxed{f(x)=\begin{cases}x&\text{if }x\ge0\\xe(x)&\text{if }x<0\end{cases}}
$$

where $e:\mathbb{R}^-\to\{-1,1\}$ is any function, these do satisfy the equation.
