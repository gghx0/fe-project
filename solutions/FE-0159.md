This is equivalent to additive + involution. (<=) is clear.

For (=>), $y=0$ gives bijective. $x=0$ gives $f(f(0)) = f(0)$, so $f(0) = 0$ by injectivity. $y=0$ again gives involution. $P(f(x),y/f(x))$ gives additivity, keeping in mind $x=0$ is already covered.

This has pathological solutions: see #6.
